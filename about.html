About Us

Re:CYCLOPES is a organization dedicated to promoting sustainable waste management practices and environmental conservation in Myanmar. Our mission is to educate individuals on smart waste management, promote eco-friendly and sustainable living and smart technologies, and engage communities in conservation-reducing pollution and improving public health in Myanmar.

Our Goal

Our goal is to encourage people in Myanmar to practice responsible waste management and adopt sustainable habits in daily life. Through education, awareness campaigns, and smart solutions, we hope to reduce pollution and create cleaner communities.

Our Vision

We envision a cleaner, healthier Myanmar where communities thrive in harmony with nature. By embracing smart technologies, sustainable practices, and collective responsibility, we aspire to create a future free from pollution—where every individual contributes to protecting the environment and safeguarding public health for generations to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are we? We are a group of Student Devs who want to volunteer their time and energy to spread and raise awareness about the waste management in Yangon, MM.
Why are we creating this website? We created this website to raise awareness about waste management issues in Myanmar and to share practical solutions that people can apply in daily life.
What is waste management? Waste management is the process of collecting, separating, recycling, and disposing of waste properly to reduce environmental pollution.
What is the waste issue in Myanmar? Myanmar faces problems such as plastic pollution, illegal dumping, poor waste collection systems, and limited recycling facilities, especially in urban areas.
Is waste pollution a serious problem in Myanmar? Yes. Waste pollution affects public health, damages the environment, blocks drainage systems, and increases flooding during the rainy season.
Why should people care about waste management? Proper waste management helps keep communities clean, protects the environment, and creates a healthier future for everyone.
How can we solve waste problems in Myanmar? Waste problems can be reduced through recycling, reducing plastic use, improving waste collection systems, public education, and community participation.
How can students help reduce waste? Students can help by using reusable items, separating waste, joining clean-up activities, and spreading awareness in schools and communities.
Is the information on this website trustworthy? Yes. The information on this website is based on research from reliable environmental sources, reports, and awareness campaigns related to waste management.
What is the goal of this website? The goal of this website is to educate people about waste issues in Myanmar and encourage sustainable actions to reduce pollution.
Can small actions really make a difference? Yes. Small actions like reducing plastic use, recycling, and proper waste disposal can create a positive environmental impact when many people participate together.
