in Yangon

Issue of Plastic Waste Pollution in Yangon

Plastic waste has become one of the biggest environmental problems in Yangon. Many people use single-use plastics such as plastic bags, cups, bottles, and food containers every day. Because of improper disposal systems and limited recycling facilities, much of this plastic waste ends up on streets, in rivers, and in open dumping areas.

In our city Yangon, plastic waste blocks drainage systems and creates unsanitary conditions. Many people also burn plastic waste, which releases toxic chemicals into the air.

What are the Effect?

Enviromental Effects

Long-term damage to natural habitats and biodiversity loss

Pollution of rivers, lakes, and oceans, making water ecosystems unsafe for plants and animals

Harm to marine life, as animals often mistake plastic for food or get trapped in it

Soil contamination caused by toxic chemicals released from decomposing plastics

Increased flooding in cities due to blocked drainage systems filled with plastic waste

Health Effects

Toxic smoke from burning plastics leads to serious respiratory problems such as asthma and bronchitis.

Polluted water sources increase the risk of diseases like diarrhea, cholera, and skin infections

Breeding of mosquitoes and pests in waste areas spreads diseases such as dengue fever and malaria

Harmful microplastics entering the food chain can affect long-term human health
