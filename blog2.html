Open Dumping and Landfill Problems

Open Dumping and Landfill Problems in Yangon

Open Landfill in Dala Town

In many parts of Myanmar, waste is disposed of in open dumping sites instead of properly managed landfills. These dumping areas often contain mixed waste including food waste, plastics, chemicals, and other harmful materials.

Many landfill sites are overcrowded and lack proper waste treatment systems. During rainy seasons, waste spreads into nearby communities and waterways.

What are the Effects?

Enviromental Effects

  • Waste can cause soil and groundwater contamination, making the environment unsafe for plants, animals, and people.
  • Air pollution from waste decomposition and burning can harm human health and increase environmental problems.
  • Bad odors from waste can affect nearby communities and make the area uncomfortable to live in.

Health Effects

  • In Myanmar, poor waste management can increase the spread of diseases carried by pests such as rats and mosquitoes, especially during the rainy season.
  • Open burning of waste in many communities contributes to air pollution and respiratory problems among residents.
  • People living near landfill sites and illegal dumping areas in Myanmar often face unsafe and unhealthy living conditions.

Infrastructure Effects

In Myanmar, blocked waterways caused by improperly disposed waste can lead to serious flooding during heavy rainfall. Waste pollution also damages local environments and makes public areas dirty, unhealthy, and unpleasant for communities.
