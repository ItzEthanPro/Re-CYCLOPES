ISSUE 4

Lack of Recycling
Awareness

What Are We Facing?

One of the major environmental issues in Yangon is the lack of recycling awareness among residents. Many people do not fully understand the importance of separating recyclable waste such as plastic, paper, glass, and metal from general trash. Because of limited public education and weak recycling habits, recyclable materials are often mixed with regular waste and thrown away improperly. In Yangon, this increases the amount of waste sent to landfills and causes more pollution in streets, rivers, and neighborhoods. Many residents are also unaware of how recycling helps conserve resources and reduce environmental damage.

What Are The Effects?

Enviromental Effects

Large amounts of recyclable materials ending up in landfills instead of being reused.

Increased pollution due to unnecessary waste burning and dumping.

Overuse of natural resources because materials are not recycled properly.

Higher carbon emissions from producing new materials instead of recycling.

Social Effects

Children growing up without knowledge of sustainability may continue harmful environmental practices in the future.

Limited environmental awareness in many areas of Myanmar leads to careless waste disposal in streets, drains, and rivers.

In some rural and urban areas, open burning of waste has become a common practice, creating unhealthy living conditions.

Poor waste management can negatively affect community health, cleanliness, and the overall quality of life in Myanmar.

How can we Contribute?

Our Mission

We aim to educate individuals on smart waste management, promote eco-friendly and sustainable living and smart technologies,
and engage communities in conservation-reducing pollution and improving public health in Myanmar.
